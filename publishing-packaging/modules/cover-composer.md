# Cover Composer

Use local deterministic composition for Chinese title text after the background exists. The background model must not render final Chinese text. Default treatment is clean title space plus legible text/stroke; borders, dark title boards, Series badges, and brand devices are opt-in only when an approved Series rule requires them. Export platform-specific files from independent composition specs, and record bounding boxes, safe zones, font file, colors, source checksum, output checksum, and platform identity.

OCR is an independent readback. Embedded composition metadata proves what the compositor attempted, not what OCR or a human can read. Missing OCR stays `unknown` and withholds Ready.
