# Storyboard and Keyframe Planner

For VOX Poster planning, consume the joint Beat–Shot–Poster use map. A representative Contact Sheet previews selected static layouts and reading tasks; it does not create a Poster or generation request for every narration sentence or semantic event. A planned source ID may guide Base/Overlay candidate design under a valid upstream baseline, but only a real versioned asset and complete Poster Preview Review can become the formal static baseline. Shared Poster media may appear in several Shot uses with separate layout/Review scope.

Storyboard validates spatial and action feasibility rather than requiring a full image for every Shot. It is optional for low risk, recommended for medium risk, and required for high-risk multi-character blocking, fast action, complex space, high-energy multi-shot plans, or important transitions.

For VOX Poster-first production, the same previsualization owner may compile a risk-selected `VOX Poster Contact Sheet` from 3–6 representative Poster Shots before batch assets or motion. It reviews whole-video editorial vocabulary and rhythm, not action alone. Named risks include editorial repetition, poster hierarchy failure, visual-rhythm monotony, critical-text crowding, missing asset coverage, static-reconstruction drift, and poster-to-motion infeasibility. The representative set should include at least one actually used decomposition/reconstruction mode when such work exists, plus any selective high-quality hero poster whose whole/partial treatment sets the visual ceiling. This remains `previsualization_storyboard`; it is not a new Gate or state owner.

Run this planner inside Skill 4 only after Stage 1 approval and Scene/Clip planning. It is not a new Skill 3-to-4 state owner. Reuse the frozen Script, ADP, Scene/Clip refs, Production Manifest, Review Result, and Execution State; never create a Creative Handoff Snapshot.

## Applicability

Record `skip` only when production risk is low and no named trigger exists. Medium/high risk, multi-character blocking, complex space, important transitions, high-energy multi-shot plans, continuity-sensitive Turning Point/Payoff, a new visual template, or high-cost error propagation requires `retain` or human review. Store concrete reasons and evidence rather than a genre label.

## Risk-selected Plan

Do not require one Panel per Shot. Select only Panels that reduce a named risk or carry an explicitly assigned Hook, Turning Point, Payoff, spatial relation, action transition, or continuity handshake. Six, nine, and twelve are layout suggestions, not schema values.

Each Panel records its ID, Script/ADP/Scene/Clip refs, time range, narrative function, assigned story-function flags, named risk, visual event, composition, character position, action/expression, environment, mood, continuity notes, and allowed non-realistic aids. Upstream assignments are references; Storyboard may not invent or rewrite motives, choices, plot, or conclusions.

## Contact Sheet and repair

Compile one Contact Sheet first with [the Storyboard template](../templates/storyboard-contact-sheet-prompt.md). It must preserve reading order and identity/spatial anchors, simplify detail, exclude provider syntax, and stop after one sheet. A local compiler or Fixture never calls a provider.

### Project anchors assembly

Compile the existing `Project anchors / CONSISTENCY_ANCHORS` in this order:

1. Preserve the selected Panels' applicable identity, spatial, prop, screen-direction, and continuity anchors first.
2. Then append only the non-duplicative, cross-Panel Story Direction anchors that reduce a named Storyboard risk: `Dramatic task`, `Visual progression`, `Emotional progression`, and/or `Interpretation guardrails`. These are optional (`0–4`), not required slots.

Derive a `Dramatic task` only by compacting the frozen Script, ADP semantic locks, and assigned story functions; do not rewrite Hook, Reveal, conclusion, facts, motives, or choices. A referenced Topic Thesis may assist checking when already available, but its absence neither blocks planning nor creates a new input requirement. Derive `Visual progression` only from the selected Beats' observable visual/material/spatial state and `expected_end_state`; do not create a parallel state in the Style Blueprint. Derive `Emotional progression` only from existing Beat performance or approved performance plans; ordinary action is not evidence of an invented motivation, hesitation, choice, or psychological turn. Collect `Interpretation guardrails` only for real, current cross-Panel misreading risks, not a generic negative-prompt list.

Omit any direction anchor with no reliable upstream evidence, no cross-Panel value, or content already covered by a consistency anchor. Keep Panel-specific constraints in that Panel's existing continuity notes, visual event, action/expression, or lighting/mood; never elevate them into Project anchors or copy them to other Panels. Source conflict, semantic ambiguity, or a needed story change routes upstream or to human review rather than being repaired in Storyboard. Keep this as concise natural language, not a schema or fixed template.

Only a Review with a named `must_fix`, evidence, Panel ID, repair target, and owner may request a Single Panel repair. Preserve every non-target Panel by reference. A real Contact Sheet or repair call always requires a separate Cost Gate.

## Evidence and Review

Prompt preview, local file creation, or `fixture_only=true` remains `planned_awaiting_cost_gate` and cannot become READY. A real Storyboard Review requires immutable asset revision, media checksum, evidence refs, and current dependency hashes. Reuse Review Result v2.1 `previsualization_storyboard`; `PASS` projects to READY, a named non-semantic repair projects to REVISE, missing/stale evidence blocks without retry consumption, and semantic impact routes to human review.

For a VOX Poster Shot or Poster Contact Sheet, add Poster Readiness findings for primary attention, editorial hierarchy, paper-layer separation, critical-text protection, no-motion readability, asset coverage, adjacent-shot distinction, Style Baseline consistency, applicable route-specific review, applicable Static Reconstruction fidelity, and poster-to-motion feasibility. Static Reconstruction compares a no-motion output against the approved poster and protects composition, hierarchy, focal weight, negative space, palette and typography character without requiring pixel-perfect identity. A pixel diff is supporting evidence only. Any unresolved `must_fix` blocks motion compilation for the affected Shot. Three highly similar consecutive Poster Shots emit `editorial_repetition_warning`; escalate it to `must_fix` only when the repetition materially harms comprehension or pacing. Variation is not a quota.

Review `hero_key_art` for visual impact, focal hierarchy, Style fidelity, emotional value, and micro-animation/video-reference feasibility. Mark Production Reconstructability findings `not_applicable`; fusion or difficulty of decomposition alone is not failure.

Review `production_reconstructable` for Visual Quality and Poster Readiness plus `typography_split_test`, `context_separation_test`, `decorative_independence_test`, `rectangle_risk_test`, and `motion_sequence_test`. The motion sequence must permit a readable state, staged element reveal/information build, and a stable poster state rather than only whole-image Zoom/Pan. A visible horizontal/vertical crop line, background halo, full-width context strip, title-plus-background rectangle, or adjacent-element fragment is `must_fix`. When visual quality passes but strong fusion makes those checks fail, the Review may recommend `hero_key_art`; preserve it through existing salvage/reuse and do not hard-crop or treat reclassification as Production PASS.

The same Review also checks that critical-text realization is one of `remotion_native_text`, `verified_typography_svg`, or `verified_typography_png`; Hero Typography is not silently replaced by ordinary CSS; historical/cultural hero numerals remain locale-appropriate without fact drift; background recovery covers only evidenced motion-exposure regions; and runtime outline treatment remains background/role/delivery-resolution aware with a separate shadow. These are existing `poster_readiness` or `editability` findings, not a Reconstruction Gate, Typography Manifest, or new state.

For a Shot with `historical_visual_mode`, add Historical VOX findings to the same Review Result. At mobile size, verify that historical-subject identity, primary title, direction/sequence, and primary evidence remain readable without zooming into decorative texture. `hero_cinematic` must keep the subject as a historical narrative anchor; `editorial_explainer` must make its map/route/time/relationship answer an explicit question; `atmospheric_historical` must establish place, era, or pressure rather than become unfocused scenery. Mark overloaded scrapbook, decorative archival clutter, unexplained map/arrow/timeline graphics, sticker-like people, competing persistent accents, material/period mismatch, critical text baked into generated imagery, or project-specific constants leaked into Style Core as `must_fix`. This reuses `previsualization_storyboard`; it adds no Historical Gate, Manifest, state machine, retry owner, or separate Planner.

For selected `oriental_pastoral_cinematic_lifestyle` craft montage, use the same Storyboard Plan and `previsualization_storyboard` Review Result. Confirm that the representative Panels include `atmosphere_shot`, `process_detail_shot`, and `progression_shot` only when those functions are present in the approved adaptive plan; do not manufacture a missing function to satisfy a template. Check that an atmosphere Panel has a named breathing, regional, emotional, contrast, visual-echo, or aftertaste function; a process-detail Panel shows a credible bounded action without claiming a full tutorial loop; and a progression Panel binds prior/current material states with a visible `completion_delta`. Five or six consecutive same-function craft macros, “beauty + environment + finished product” without credible making evidence, portrait-studio staging, tourism/product-promo drift, magic growth, full-frame morph, or plastic 3D craft output is `must_fix`. Review the chosen rhythm against the current narrative, process state, information density, human/space relationship, music or emotional arc, and breathing need; no fixed ratio, default sequence, or mandatory alternation is valid by itself.

Storyboard PASS advances only to Prompt planning. It does not approve a Visual Baseline, Prompt Preflight, Production generation, actual continuity, or publishing.

Use First Frame, End Frame, Transition Frame, and Continuity Resume Frame only where they reduce a named risk. A Resume Keyframe inherits inspected `actual_end_state`, not the ideal ADP state.

`Scene Setting` is a plot-free spatial baseline for topology, fixed objects, and coverage. `Key Frame` is a story/action reference. `Resume Frame` is a continuity reference derived from the ledger's observed state. These roles are not interchangeable and a Fixture never upgrades one role into another.

For VOX Beat previsualization, show the information goal and changing primary attention across the necessary key states, including a changed composition when that improves understanding. Review applicable before-event, handoff, after-event and stable Poster frames; an intentional empty prelude is not a failed final Poster, and a good ending does not excuse a broken intermediate state. Separate positioning anchors, fixed explanation, evidence labels, conclusions and subtitles by project strategy; verify facts, glyphs and no early conclusion reveal. A whole-poster/independent-element handoff uses one coordinate system and mutually exclusive display at the switch frame.

For VOX natural-contour assembly, inspect before-element empty background, handoff/intermediate states, movement extremes and the stable Poster. Verify original visible outlines/hair/robes/capes, complete map context, contact edges, margins and attached text. New outlines are not mandatory when natural separation reads; an approved intrinsic outline must not be clipped or doubled. Still comparisons establish static feasibility only. Perceptible layered parallax, temporal texture and continuous-to-tail intent require final encoded playback in existing production QA; no static panel or last-frame pixel difference substitutes for that review.
