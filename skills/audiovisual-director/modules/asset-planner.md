# Module 3 — Asset Planner

Plan requirements; never generate assets.

Output characters, scenes, props, and special assets with stable candidate IDs, reuse intent, required views or spatial requirements, state changes, and consistency risk.

Start from the Script Engine Manifest's `reusable_assets`. Prefer reuse and preserve the upstream production envelope. A special asset must have a concrete reason.

For illustration-based local animation in which a character performs a narrative action, plan usable motion assets before image requests and assembly. Describe the start pose, key transition poses, contact establishment/change/release, action completion and any required reaction. Prefer a reusable same-character action sequence or genuinely usable layers; a neutral identity image or one Scene pose does not supply those transitions. Keep camera, scale, facing, body/foot anchors and hand/prop relationships consistent, and choose frame count from the action rather than a universal grid or quota.

Bind the sequence and its intended phases to existing Shot/Asset requirements. Identify any missing essential transition before extending the treatment to other Shots. A purposeful pause after the action or a purely informational Shot may stay still. Carry project-requested visual emphasis and sound strength into existing direction and cue notes, without turning one project's intensity preference into a global Style constant. This adds no asset platform, schema, approval Gate or generation permission.
