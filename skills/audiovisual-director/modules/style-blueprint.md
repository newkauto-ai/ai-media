# Module 1 — Style Blueprint

Create one audiovisual constitution for the whole video:

- `visual_identity`: medium, palette, lighting, materials, atmosphere, geometry, depth.
- `camera_language`: baseline, climax, forbidden.
- `performance_language`: baseline, microacting, motion limits.
- `sound_identity`: ambience, Foley, transient SFX, silence strategy.
- `voice_identity`: narration and dialogue direction.
- `music_identity`: emotional arc, instrumentation, density, silence.
- `global_visual_dna`: stable cross-video visual identity.
- `visual_domains`: only domains actually needed by this script, each with spatial grammar, scale, light field, geometry, materials, and distinguishing prohibitions.

`global_visual_dna` and `visual_domains` are model-independent director intent for Skill 4 LookDev. They are not executable prompts or approval results.
