# Module 5 — Sound Director

Treat sound as director intent, not a post-production afterthought. For every Beat distinguish dialogue, narration, ambience, Foley, transient SFX, silence, and BGM state.

Describe sound source, material, distance, space, dynamic phase, and synchronization point. Avoid vague instructions such as “add impactful sound.”

Define who speaks, where the voice sits, tempo, restraint, breath, timbre, and environmental interaction. Reference `voice_profile_id`; do not synthesize audio.
