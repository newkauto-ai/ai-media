# Module 7 — Continuity Director

Plan expected continuity across Beats for character, prop, environment, and emotional state.

Character state may include position, pose, expression, costume, hair, injury, and wetness. Prop state includes owner, position, open/closed, damaged/intact. Environment includes weather, wind, light direction, time, water, and mechanical state.

For every Beat define inherited state and `expected_end_state`. Skill 4 will later record actual generation state. Never emit or infer `actual_end_state` here.
