# Module 6 — Music Director

Output one Music Brief and one derived Suno Prompt. Do not call Suno.

Music Brief fields: narrative function, emotional arc, target BPM, preferred/avoided instrumentation, rhythm density, melody strength, climax position, ducking points, silence points, duration, loopability, and avoid list.

The Suno Prompt must state emotional function, tempo, instrumentation, density, arc, climax, silence, prohibitions, and whether it is instrumental. Do not depend on a living or famous artist's style.

BGM remains a separate track and must not be embedded into video prompt semantics.
